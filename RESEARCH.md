# Market research

Dated so it can be re-checked rather than trusted forever. Everything here is
from published market write-ups, not from this shop's own sales — it has none
yet, so nothing below is validated by our own data.

**Reviewed: 12 August 2026**

---

## What the price band data says

The $10–30 band converts best, and the reason given is consistent everywhere:
low price means low hesitation and a fast checkout. US TikTok Shop beauty sales
were **$662.2M in Q2 2026** at an **average price of $26.90**.

Our catalogue sits at **$14–34** with two bundles above it. That is the right
band, and the average price is close to the category average. No change needed.

### The gap: fragrance

Fragrance is 19% of the largest category on the platform and this shop sells
none. It also fits the model well — high margin, small and light to ship,
strongly gift-driven in November and December.

**Recommended, not done.** Adding SKUs now would make an existing problem worse:
27 products are already listed with no supplier and no real cost behind them.
Sourcing what exists comes before widening the range. Revisit once the first two
supplier accounts are open.

### Second gap: makeup and hair

Makeup is 28% of beauty and we sell one lip product. Hair is called out as a
top-three opportunity and we have two items. Both are worth one or two additions
each — again, after sourcing.

## Where this shop is already aligned

- LED aesthetic lighting keeps performing; the Sunset Projection Lamp is exactly
  that product, and one comparable $15 galaxy projector reportedly took 10,000
  orders in two weeks off a single creator video.
- Pet and home gadgets are named repeatedly; both are represented.
- Electronics stay excluded on purpose — highest revenue, thinnest margin, worst
  return rates.

## Sourcing note that changes the plan

**Faire** is now the first candidate for home, kitchen, pet, fitness and
wellness. Retailer minimums are roughly $100–200 per brand, and it offers
**net-60 terms** — the stock arrives and is paid for 60 days later, which means a
first order can be sold before it is paid for. For a shop starting without
capital that matters more than a few points of margin.

Note the commission figures in most articles (25% new, 15% repeat) are what Faire
charges **brands selling on it**, not retailers buying from it.

**Abound is gone.** It was acquired by Carro in early 2024 and the wholesale
marketplace no longer exists, though it still appears in most "Faire
alternatives" listicles. Do not spend time on it.

## Honest limits of this research

- No supplier prices are in here, because those sit behind account logins.
- Figures come from published market summaries and none were verifiable against
  a primary source from this environment.
- The trend data describes TikTok Shop specifically. This shop is a standalone
  site, so treat it as a signal about what people are buying, not a forecast of
  what this site will sell.
- Viral products have short commercial lives — commonly quoted as 8–16 weeks.
  Anything bought deep on the strength of a trend is a bet on that window.
